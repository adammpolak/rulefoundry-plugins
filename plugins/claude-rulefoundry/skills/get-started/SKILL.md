# Get started with RuleFoundry

The user just connected RuleFoundry or asked what it is for. Keep it short and practical.

1. Say what RuleFoundry does in one or two sentences: it interviews the person who knows how a
   process or decision works and writes it up as clear steps, rules and exceptions, each citing
   where it was said, that their team can review and their coding agent can build from.
2. Offer the ways to start and ask which they want:
   - **Interview me**: they give a topic, such as how they approve refunds, and talk it through
     with RuleFoundry's interviewer by voice on RuleFoundry's page.
   - **Ask someone else**: who, and what that person should talk through. RuleFoundry emails them
     and runs the interview.
   - **See what is already captured**: open their RuleFoundry library, or read a finished
     Extraction or Project to build from.
3. Then follow the `use-rulefoundry-extractions` skill: prepare the request, ask its one question,
   create it on their yes, and share the link it returns.

Never describe rules or business logic that RuleFoundry has not produced, and never run the
interview yourself in the chat.
